Cambridge 9618 · AS Level

A focused preparation plan

Start with your weakest topics, practise retrieval, then test under timed conditions. Adapt this rolling plan to your confirmed exam date and syllabus.

Repeat each week

1 · Diagnose and learn

Attempt a mixed paper without notes. Record lost marks by syllabus section. Use the Paper 1 and Paper 2 notes to revisit the two weakest areas, then explain each idea from memory.

2 · Practise algorithms

Translate a problem into structured English, then pseudocode. Trace normal, boundary and invalid cases. Alternate linear search, bubble sort, arrays, files and subroutines.

3 · Apply under time

Complete timed questions from both papers. Mark against the specific mark scheme; log the missing point, the correct reasoning and a follow-up date.

4 · Repair and revisit

Redo missed questions without looking after a day and again the following week. If the same error recurs, revise the concept and try a fresh question.

Paper 2 checklist

  • Use ← for assignment and = for comparison; use & for string concatenation.
  • Check array bounds and loop termination; trace the first and last iterations.
  • Use quoted literal filenames, pair OPENFILE with CLOSEFILE and check EOF before reading. Convert between strings and numbers when required.
  • Choose BYREF when a caller's variable must be changed; distinguish procedures from functions and return a value where required.
  • Write a sensible partial solution if stuck, but do not assume a header or declaration earns a fixed number of marks: credit depends on the question's mark scheme.
